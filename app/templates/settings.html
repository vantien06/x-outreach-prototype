How sending works on X

Checked against the live X API on 14 Nov 2026. These are rules of X, not settings of this tool.

  • Set up X Chat on the sending account first. Open Messages on x.com as @{{ account.handle }} and finish the passcode step once. In our test, messages sent before that step only showed up for the recipient afterwards.
  • First messages land in Message requests. If the recipient does not follow you, your DM waits in their Requests tab until they accept. Some people never look there.
  • X can refuse a DM. If the recipient does not accept requests from accounts like yours, X rejects it. The message is marked Blocked with the reason and is never retried.
  • Replies are encrypted. This tool sees who replied and when, and shows "Replied (encrypted)". Read and answer the reply in the conversation on X; each reply here links straight to it.
  • One message per person, ever. If this account already has a conversation with someone, nothing is sent to them.

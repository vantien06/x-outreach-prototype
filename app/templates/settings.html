How sending works on X

A few things X decides, so you know what to expect.

  • Set up your X chat passcode first. Open Messages on x.com as @{{ account.handle }} and create your passcode once. Until you do, your messages may not reach the other person.
  • A first message arrives as a request. If the person does not follow you, your message waits in their Message requests until they accept it. Not everyone checks that tab.
  • Some people cannot be messaged. Many accounts only accept messages from people they follow. You will see "DMs closed to you" before drafting, and a message X refuses is listed under Blocked and never retried.
  • Replies are private. X encrypts replies, so here you see who answered and when. Open the conversation on X to read it and write back.
  • One message per person. Nobody is messaged twice, and nothing is sent to someone you already have a conversation with.

Disconnect and delete my data

This removes the tool's access to your X account and deletes your campaigns, the people it found, drafts, the sent list and your sign-in. One thing is kept: an unreadable fingerprint of each person you already messaged, so that if you connect again nobody is messaged a second time.
